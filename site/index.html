Definitions

Rollout
A trained model trajectory simulated from the validation flight initial condition and control inputs.
Model eval s
Wall-clock time to simulate the trained model over the validation trajectories; it is not the flight duration.
Validation score
Normalized held-out trajectory error over the available state or pose channels; lower is better, but absolute pose RMSE should be checked for long trajectories.
